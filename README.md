# Connected Records: The Visual Atlas of the Epstein Files

Connected Records is a visual research atlas tracing documented institutional, financial, academic, research, policy, conference, and funding connections found across Epstein-related records.

The project organizes information from government records, university reviews, foundation filings, institutional reports, correspondence, financial disclosures, conference materials, and related source documents.

The research keeps different evidence types separate. Payments, proposals, introductions, reimbursements, institutional roles, research funding, grant commitments, meetings, and unresolved claims are not treated as interchangeable.

## What the project maps

The visual atlas traces connections involving:

- government funding and oversight
- private foundation funding
- academic and scientific institutions
- donor relationships
- research programs
- conferences and policy development
- institutional leadership
- financial flows
- documented introductions and intermediary relationships

## Research approach

The underlying research was built by tracing records across institutions and time, then organizing the results into visual maps and source-indexed research sections.

A central objective is to make complex systems easier to inspect without collapsing different kinds of evidence into a single category.
